# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in technical discussion.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give and accept feedback without personal attacks.
- Protect private information and use synthetic data in examples and reports.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting features. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe, productive project environment. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
